About This Page

This page demonstrates five jQuery interactions built as part of my web development coursework. Each exercise showcases a different aspect of jQuery. These skills strengthen me as a digital marketer by giving me a stronger foundation in website development and interactive user experience design.

Interaction 1 — Show / Hide

Toggle the Marketing Tip

Click to reveal or hide a quick marketing insight. Uses jQuery's .slideToggle() for a smooth animated effect.

Pro Tip: In lifecycle email marketing, segmenting your audience by engagement tier, active, lapsing, and dormant,can increase open rates. Personalized subject lines and dynamic content blocks are key to re-engagement journeys.
Interaction 2 — Fade Effect

Fade In a Career Quote

Click the button to fade in a favorite professional quote. Uses jQuery's .fadeToggle() method.

Interaction 3 — Dynamic List

Build My Skills List 0
